# eTender Guru — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product Overview

eTender Guru is an established Indian business focused on education, training, guidance, and advisory support related to Government Tenders, e-Procurement, Government contracting, and the Government e-Marketplace (GeM).

The organization helps individuals, entrepreneurs, businesses, contractors, engineers, MSMEs, service providers, women entrepreneurs, self-help groups, and other eligible participants understand and navigate the Government Tender ecosystem.

The new website will become the primary digital presence of eTender Guru.

The website should clearly communicate what eTender Guru does, who it serves, what people can learn, what training and webinar opportunities are available, and how visitors can get in touch or register.

The website should be informative, credible, professional, modern, approachable, and built around the real business rather than generic marketing language.

---

# 2. Business Background

Government procurement and tendering can be difficult for people who are unfamiliar with the process.

People entering this ecosystem may have questions about:

- What a Government Tender is
- Different types of tenders
- Who can participate
- Eligibility requirements
- Required documentation
- Tender procedures
- e-Procurement
- Government procurement
- GeM
- How businesses can participate
- How tendering can become part of a business strategy
- What precautions should be taken
- How payments and post-tender processes work

eTender Guru provides education and practical guidance intended to make these subjects easier to understand.

The organization operates around the idea of helping people become more informed and capable participants in the Government Tender ecosystem.

# 27. Product Development Philosophy

The website should be developed as a real product, not as a single AI-generated landing-page output.

The process should be iterative:

1. Understand the business
2. Establish the foundation
3. Build a small part
4. Review the actual result
5. Refine
6. Continue to the next part
7. Perform content, responsive, accessibility, SEO, and performance QA
8. Prepare for final launch

Quality is more important than generating a large quantity of UI at once.
